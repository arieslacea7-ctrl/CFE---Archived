ABS // THE 10 DIRECTIVES

  1. REPORT TO A HIGH RANK

    Report bullying to an authorized CFE high rank or trusted teacher.

  2. USE YOUR SPY ADVANTAGES

    Stay observant and record relevant facts safely. Never hack accounts or invade privacy.

  3. PROTECT THE VICTIM

    Offer support and help the victim find someone they trust.

  4. PRESERVE THE EVIDENCE

    Keep relevant screenshots or messages you can legitimately access.

  5. MAINTAIN CONFIDENTIALITY

    Share reports only with people authorized to help.

  6. DO NOT RETALIATE

    No revenge, threats, or counter-bullying.

  7. VERIFY BEFORE REPORTING

    Tell the truth and never invent evidence.

  8. STOP GROUP HARASSMENT

    Never join dogpiling, mocking, or coordinated attacks.

  9. FOLLOW THE CHAIN OF COMMAND

    Contact an authorized high rank. If they are involved, contact a trusted teacher.

  10. PRIORITIZE SAFETY

    Get school staff or emergency help if someone is in immediate danger.
